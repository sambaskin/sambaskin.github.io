City Animations

I created this series of animations to brush up on After Effects while I had been primarily working in Illustrator. I used Photoshop and After Effects for most of these. I shot all the footage on my phone to give it an everyday look, while also making it easy to collect footage whenever inspiration struck. In all of these projects I used motion tracking since I filmed the source footage with no tripod. I also added sound effects to make these clips seem more realistic.
