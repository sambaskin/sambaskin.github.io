Work / 3484 Magazine · Motion design

3484 Promotional Videos

Promo videos I made for 3484 Magazine across several issues, mostly by animating each issue's cover in After Effects for Instagram.

Spring 2020 Promo

After Effects · Photoshop

I created this video using Adobe Photoshop and After Effects. The goal was to make an animated version of the issue's cover photo that could be used to promote the magazine. I used After Effects to animate the still cover photo, adding blinking lights, a moving plane, a spinning ferris wheel, and visual noise. Then I used an animated mask to reveal the skyline outline. The background noise was a mix of ambient recordings I took around Atlanta.

Fall 2020 Promo

After Effects · Photoshop

The Fall 2020 promo video was created from original footage which I edited with Adobe Photoshop and After Effects. Originally, the lamp post in the video had a 3484 sticker and the number "43." I used Photoshop to edit an in-focus frame, replacing the sticker with the Spring cover voter sticker and changing the 43 to 3484. Then I matched the movement and focus in After Effects.

Climate Issue Promo

After Effects · Photoshop

My goal was to create a short promotional video we could post on Instagram to get people excited about the upcoming issue. I animated the original cover Photoshop file in After Effects and then added sound effects. The tree ring animation also highlights the Atlanta skyline in the stump.

Connections Issue Promo

After Effects · Photoshop

I animated the original cover Photoshop file in After Effects and then added sound effects. The cover features an aerial cutout of Georgia Tech's campus, surrounded by the Atlanta skyline.

3484 2021 Animation

Stop motion · Cut-outs

I created this animation to bring attention to 3484 Magazine and to explain the magazine to new readers. I wanted to create a video that was visually interesting, shareable, and informative. Using cut-outs from various issues of the magazine, I recreated a similar style from my Sam's Neat Projects YouTube channel. We didn't have a budget for this video, so I used a homemade overhead rig with basic lighting in my bedroom.
