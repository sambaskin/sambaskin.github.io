Personal Work

Music videos and projects I've made on my own time.

Amarlude Music Video

CD-ROM COM · Animation

I created this music video for CD-ROM COM, an Atlanta-based rock/funk band. CD-ROM COM gave me a lot of artistic freedom, so I decided that this would be a good opportunity to expand on the style I used in my previous animation series. I wanted to add motion to iconography that people see every day, so I recorded short clips of street signs around Brooklyn. I used After Effects, Photoshop, and the character animation plug-in Rubberhose. I also included an animated montage in the middle of the video to make sure the sign animations didn't get too repetitive.

Fridge Town

Illustration · Stop motion

This is a fun personal project I started in my apartment. I drew buildings and roads onto isometric patterns that I printed onto magnet sheets, which allowed all the buildings to be easily connected with the same scale and perspective. After drawing a building I would cut it out and put it on our fridge. I documented each new addition, and even got friends and roommates to add to it too. Once I had enough pictures I turned it into a gif to show its progress over time. I documented the process in a thread on Twitter.
